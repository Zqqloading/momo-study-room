The document is materially better than round 1: the question mix, partial scoring, per-concept score, YAML/SQLite split, misconception promotion/reopening, review state machine, completion definitions, PDF phase, source fields, permission boundary, and ASR test set are all now written down. But the fixes introduced contradictions in the same core loop they were meant to repair:

1. The review queue is both derived from `concept_session_score` and exited by a review outcome that is excluded from that score (N-01).
2. A `kind=review` session has zero questions, so it violates the 5-row invariant and cannot satisfy the completion rule, while being counted for the day (N-02).
3. No flow produces the second and third concept sessions that the mastery formula expects, and the 40-node target cannot coexist with a three-session window (N-03).
4. A skipped prerequisite permanently locks its descendants (N-04).
5. The misconception trigger requires "next session", but the re-ask block only runs when a review is due (N-05).
6. The 2.5 s budget assigns 0.6 s to upload plus ASR of a multi-minute explanation (N-06).

The minimum before freeze: resolve N-01 through N-06, remove the L80 and L358 stale sentences, register the 4 s latency fallback in TBD, add the missing intermediate node target or delete "中间目标", and make TBD the only place the weekly hours appear. The remaining minor findings (N-07 through N-17) should be dispositioned at the same time or explicitly deferred with a phase.
